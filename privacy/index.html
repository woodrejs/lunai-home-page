1. Who is the data controller?

The controller of your personal data is the creator and operator of the Lunai app, based in Poland / the European Union. For privacy-related inquiries, you can contact us at: lunai.mobile.app@gmail.com

2. What data do we collect and why?

We collect only the data necessary to provide, secure, personalize, analyze, and improve the Lunai service. This may include your email address for account identification, authentication, communication, and account recovery; your username or display name, which may be a pseudonym; your country or region, if provided, to improve localization and service quality; and dream descriptions together with AI-generated interpretations, analyses, and visualizations stored as part of your account history.

We may also collect device identifiers, technical information about your device and application environment, and information about how you interact with the app, such as screens viewed, features used, and actions performed. These data may be used for app functionality, analytics, security, service improvement, advertising attribution, campaign measurement, and marketing performance analysis.

Data is processed on the basis of Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract), Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interests), Article 6(1)(c) GDPR (legal obligations), or your consent where required by law.

3. Analytics, advertising attribution and tracking

We use analytics services to understand how users interact with the app, improve existing features, identify technical issues, and develop new functionality.

We use Aptabase to collect product analytics, including information about app usage, feature interactions, device characteristics, and application performance.

We may also use TikTok SDK and related services to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns, attribute app installs and conversions, improve marketing performance, and analyze app interaction events. Depending on your device settings, permissions, and consent choices, TikTok may receive information such as device identifiers, app interaction events, technical data, and other information required for attribution, analytics, and campaign measurement.

Where required by Apple or applicable law, tracking technologies are used only after obtaining the user's consent through the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework or another legally required consent mechanism. You can withdraw or change your tracking permission at any time in your device settings.

4. Where do we store data and for how long?

Servers are located within the EU where possible. Some service providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. In such cases, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses or other mechanisms permitted under applicable data protection law.

Account data is retained for the duration of account activity and, following deletion, for the period required by law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. Dream history is deleted upon permanent account deletion, unless longer retention is required by law.

5. Who do we share data with?

Your data may be shared only with trusted service providers that help us operate, secure, analyze, and improve the app. These may include our cloud infrastructure and hosting providers; external AI model providers, only to the extent necessary to generate dream interpretations, analyses, and visualizations; Aptabase for product analytics and application performance monitoring; TikTok Technology Limited, TikTok Information Technologies UK Limited, and/or their affiliated companies for advertising attribution, campaign measurement, analytics, and marketing performance purposes; and public authorities where disclosure is required by applicable law.

We do not sell or rent your personal data.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, rectify it, erase it, restrict processing, data portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing is based on your consent, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

You may also lodge a complaint with the President of the Polish Data Protection Authority (uodo.gov.pl). To exercise any of the above rights, please contact us through the app or at: lunai.mobile.app@gmail.com.

7. Changes to this Policy

We will notify you of material changes at least 14 days in advance via an in-app notification or email. Continued use of the app after the effective date of the changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
