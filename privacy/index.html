1. Who is the data controller?

The controller of your personal data is the creator and operator of the Lunai app, based in Poland / the European Union. For privacy-related inquiries, you can contact us at: lunai.mobile.app@gmail.com

2. What data do we collect and why?

We only collect what is necessary to operate the app: your email address (for account identification and communication), username (which may be a pseudonym), country (optional, to personalize the service), and dream descriptions together with AI-generated interpretations and visualizations (stored as part of your account history). Data is processed on the basis of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) or our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3. Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics (device type, screens visited, session duration). Google may process this data outside the EEA under standard contractual clauses. You can opt out of analytics at: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

4. Where do we store data and for how long?

Servers are located within the EU. Account data is retained for the duration of account activity and, following deletion, for the period required by law (up to 5 years). Dream history is deleted immediately upon permanent account deletion.

5. Who do we share data with?

Your data may be shared only with: our cloud infrastructure provider (servers in the EU), the external AI model provider (to the extent necessary to generate interpretations), Google LLC (analytics data), and public authorities where required by law. We do not sell or rent your data.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, rectify it, erase it, restrict processing, data portability, and to object to processing. You may also lodge a complaint with the President of the Polish Data Protection Authority (uodo.gov.pl). To exercise any of the above rights, please contact us through the app.

7. Changes to this Policy

We will notify you of material changes at least 14 days in advance via an in-app notification or email. Continued use of the app after the effective date of the changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
